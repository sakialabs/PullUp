# PullUp, Short and Simple

> Homes made for chillin’ not flipping.

**Version:** 1.1  
**Status:** Shareable concept baseline  
**Date:** August 24, 2026

## What is PullUp?

PullUp is a better way to find a home, move in, and live there.

It is made for everyday people, not landlords trying to squeeze the most money out of every room.

The app feels simple and friendly, like the best modern social apps. Under the hood, it connects fair housing, co-ops, community land, repairs, shared resources, clean energy, and resident power.

Think of PullUp as:

- a dating-style discovery app, but you browse homes, not people;
- one reusable housing application;
- a home dashboard;
- a repair tracker;
- a library for shared tools, rooms, and bikes;
- a simple way to vote and take part;
- a bridge to your local community;
- a safety net when money or life changes.

## What problem does it solve?

Finding housing is stressful because:

- the real cost is often hidden;
- the same forms must be filled again and again;
- listings can be fake or misleading;
- people get ghosted;
- rent goes up while service stays bad;
- repairs disappear into a black hole;
- housing decisions feel unfair;
- useful stuff sits unused in every home;
- smart-home tech can become creepy;
- people have little power over the place they pay for.

PullUp puts the important facts in the open and gives people useful tools before and after move-in.

It also supports co-ops, nonprofits, community land trusts, public housing, and other partners that keep homes affordable for the long term.

## How does finding a home work?

### 1. Browse the Flow

The **Flow** shows available **Places**.

You can swipe if you like:

- right to **Keep** a Place;
- left for **Not It**;
- up to **Pull Up** and request a tour.

Swiping is optional. Buttons, lists, maps, keyboard, screen reader, phone, print, and human help all work too.

You swipe on Places. PullUp never swipes on people.

### 2. Know the Total Damage

**Total Damage** means the full cost of living there, not only the rent in the ad.

It can show:

- rent or housing charge;
- utilities;
- required fees;
- internet;
- deposits;
- insurance;
- parking or transport;
- move-in costs;
- real optional extras.

**Where It Goes** explains what the money pays for.

### 3. Get The Rundown

**The Rundown** is the full Place page.

It includes:

- **The Vibe:** what living there is actually like;
- **Heads Up:** honest downsides and warnings;
- **Word on the Block:** verified resident insight;
- **The Backstory:** repairs, upgrades, and inspections;
- **Around Here:** transit, groceries, parks, health care, libraries, and more;
- **Fine Print:** clear help understanding agreements and rights.

### 4. Compare your Keepers

Saved Places are called **Keepers**.

Use **Side by Side** to compare cost, space, accessibility, transport, comfort, rules, and tradeoffs.

PullUp does not tell everyone the same home is “best.” The right fit depends on your life.

### 5. Apply once

**Home Passport** is a reusable housing application that you control.

You share only what a provider needs. You can see who opened it and when.

PullUp does not create a tenant score.

**Where We At?** shows the real application status, next step, timing, and reason for important decisions. No ghosting.

### 6. Follow your Move Plan

**Move Plan** is your private checklist from tour to move-in.

It can track documents, payments, keys, utilities, address changes, furniture, accessibility needs, and move-day tasks.

## What happens after I move in?

### My Place

**My Place** is the home dashboard.

It shows costs, repairs, notices, bookings, documents, household access, and upcoming actions.

### The Setup

**The Setup** explains the rooms, appliances, heating, cooling, water, electrical systems, controls, and manuals.

You should not need a detective to find the water shutoff.

### Fixes

**Fixes** lets you report a repair, see who has it, follow updates, confirm it was solved, or reopen it.

You can use text, photo, voice, phone, or human help.

### Home Crew and Ground Rules

**Home Crew** is your household.

**Ground Rules** helps roommates or family record simple plans for quiet hours, guests, bills, cleaning, pets, food, and shared stuff.

### Commons

**Commons** is the shared library.

Depending on the building, you may borrow or book:

- tools;
- cargo bikes;
- guest rooms;
- work rooms;
- studios;
- kitchen gear;
- gardens;
- storage;
- accessibility equipment;
- shared clean energy.

The idea is simple: spend less, waste less, and get access to more.

### The Board

**The Board** shows building news, repairs, events, budgets, meetings, and decisions.

Important notices also work by phone, email, print, or in person.

## What if I need help?

### Damage Control

**Damage Control** connects people to payment plans, benefits, emergency help, utility support, financial counselling, or legal support.

It is help, not a debt-collection trick.

### Stay Put

**Stay Put** helps prevent avoidable displacement.

It can connect a person to human support, payment options, repairs, accessibility help, legal help, or a fair review.

PullUp does not use AI to recommend eviction.

### Switch Up

**Switch Up** helps someone move to a better-fit Place in the network when their household, job, health, budget, safety, or accessibility needs change.

## What is Day One?

**Day One** is the shared support feature. Your **Twin** is the one real person you trust inside it.

It might be a friend, sibling, partner, parent, roommate, neighbour, advocate, or chosen family.

You can **Twin Up** and let them:

- compare selected Places;
- join a tour;
- check one document;
- follow the Move Plan;
- track one repair;
- help with an approved payment or booking;
- **Tag Team** on tasks;
- use **Run It By Me** for a second opinion;
- **Hold It Down** for one task while you are away;
- **Double Tap** an important action with you;
- **Split It** without seeing your whole account;
- respond when you ask them to **Pull Through**;
- build **Our Move** with you.

**Max Pass** controls exactly what they can reach and when access expires. **Receipts** shows what they viewed, changed, or approved.

Day One is not an AI clone, a premium tier, or a stranger picked by the app.

## How does it connect to the other Sakia Labs apps?

### fLOKr

fLOKr is the community layer.

You can:

- see a local **Loop**;
- post or answer **Signals**;
- join **Circles**;
- work with **Crews**;
- find trusted **Leads**;
- discover paid **Shifts**;
- join **Moves** such as workshops, events, or local action.

Social activity never affects your housing, price, repairs, or rights.

### TapIn

TapIn is the shared action rail. It handles approved payments, bookings, joining, reservations, confirmations, shared costs, and temporary access.

It never gives a Twin your money or keys by default.

### Lexi

Lexi explains the Fine Print in plain language and shows sources.

It helps you understand. It does not replace a lawyer or make the final decision.

### Makana

Makana helps with the personal side of moving, routines, goals, wellbeing, and LifeMaxxxin.

Private Makana thoughts never enter housing decisions.

The big idea is:

**Find your fLOKr. PullUp to your place. TapIn.**

## Where is the AI?

Mostly out of the way.

PullUp does not need a loud AI mascot.

Quiet tech can help:

- find better-fit Places;
- translate;
- explain;
- route repairs;
- spot missing listing facts;
- predict maintenance;
- lower energy waste;
- check for unfair results.

When you need help, tap **Need a Hand?** It can open a guide, a form, your Twin, or a real person.

AI does not decide who deserves a home.

## What about smart-home gadgets?

Useful devices may:

- catch leaks;
- check air quality;
- improve heating and cooling;
- manage solar and batteries;
- warn about failing equipment;
- make controls more accessible;
- open the correct repair guide.

PullUp says no to cameras and microphones inside homes, face scanning, emotion tracking, secret occupancy tracking, data sales, and remote lockouts used as punishment.

Smart should mean safer and cheaper, not creepier.

## How does PullUp fight housing greed?

An app cannot do this alone.

PullUp must work with strong real-world models:

- community land trusts that keep land out of speculation;
- co-ops and nonprofits that own or run homes;
- cost-based monthly charges;
- secure long-term housing;
- resident voting and power;
- public land and patient funding;
- legal locks that stop future flipping;
- surplus going back into repairs, affordability, clean energy, and more homes.

PullUp learns from Vienna, Finland, Uruguay, community land trusts, Catalonia, German housing collectives, Canadian co-ops, and cooperative energy projects.

It copies the useful rules, not just the look.

## What makes it sustainable?

PullUp follows a practical solarpunk idea:

- repair before replacing;
- retrofit before demolishing when possible;
- use clean energy;
- share useful stuff;
- reduce bills and waste;
- make homes healthy and comfortable;
- let residents share the benefit.

This is not “green” marketing on top of expensive rent.

## How does PullUp make money?

PullUp can charge housing partners for software, setup, training, shared buying, and support.

It can also work through public contracts and grants.

It does not make money from:

- higher rent;
- more evictions;
- selling your data;
- ads;
- application fees;
- paid listing boosts;
- hidden charges;
- flipping homes.

## What happens first?

The best first test is a small partnership in London, Ontario:

- one trusted housing co-op or nonprofit;
- about 50 to 150 homes;
- paid resident advisors;
- strong legal, privacy, accessibility, and human-rights review;
- clear Place information;
- Fixes;
- Commons;
- The Board;
- fLOKr connection;
- one small, consent-based smart-home test.

Prove that life gets better. Then grow.

## The whole thing in one breath

PullUp helps you find a real home, see the full cost, apply once, use Day One with someone you trust, move with less stress, get repairs done, share useful resources, join your community if you want, understand your rights, protect your data, lower waste, and have a real say.

It is friendly on the screen and serious underneath.

**Your people. Your place. PullUp.**
